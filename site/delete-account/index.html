Delete your Quando? account

Quando?, Matai? and Quando Mine use one optional sync account. Deleting it erases your synced alarms, settings and connection keys from our cloud database (Google Firebase) and removes your sign-in. Alarms stored on your devices are not touched.

In the app

  1. Open Settings and tap your account.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with your password, or sign in with Google again.

Your account and cloud data are erased immediately.

Without the app

Email support@quando.day from the address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete your account and cloud data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is kept

Nothing about you is kept by us after deletion. Short-lived backups held by Google may take up to 90 days to clear. The anonymous “Help improve voice alarms” sentences carry no account or device identifier, so they cannot be linked to you or removed individually.
