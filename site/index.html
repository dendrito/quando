Everything an alarm should be.

Built around your calendar, the sun and the way you like to wake up. For Android and iPad.

⏰

Alarms that fit your day

Repeat days, labels, groups, presets, duplicate, turn off, undo and swipe to delete.

🗓️

Calendar alarms

Ring before, at or after any event in your phone calendars, Google Calendar included. Search events with AND, OR and NOT. Calendar notifications can become loud alarms.

🌅

Sunrise and sunset

Alarms tied to the sun in your city, with moon phase and weather on a living sky.

🌍

Time zones

Several world clocks and alarms in other time zones.

🎤

Say or type it

“Remind me to take my pills at 8pm every day.” Quando? turns it into an alarm, in your language.

🔒

Lock mode

Turn your phone into a quiet clock for a focused night, a day off or a calendar event.

🎵

Wake up your way

Gentle nature sounds, your own recording, online radio, or a song from Spotify or YouTube Music.

🧠

Get up for real

Solve a sum, shake the phone or scan a code to stop. Snooze limits, wake-up check and spoken alarms.

🎚️

Fine control

Volume, fade in and out, how long it rings to the second, hands-free alarms, volume-button snooze.

📋

Timers and stopwatch

Stopwatch with laps, countdown timer and spoken “get set” starts.

🔗

Connected

Pushover, Zapier, IFTTT, webhooks and Smart Life or Tuya scenes when an alarm rings.

🤫

Busy mode and widget

Quiet your notifications on schedule, plus an Android home-screen widget and a share card for your day.

Optional sync

Sign in to keep alarms and settings on all your devices. Not required.

Many languages

English, Portuguese, Spanish, Hebrew and more, with right-to-left support.
