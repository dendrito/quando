Time well kept.

Quando? is an alarm clock that knows when things actually happen. Set alarms around your meetings, sunrise and sunset, then wake up the way you like.

Ask for early access

Calendar alarms

Ring before, at or after any event in your phone's calendars, Google Calendar included. Quando? only reads events; it never changes them.

Sun and sky

Sunrise and sunset alarms, moon phase and weather for the city you choose.

Wake up your way

Nature sounds, your own recordings, online radio, or a song from Spotify or YouTube Music.

Get up for real

Solve a sum, shake the phone or scan a code to stop an alarm. Snooze limits and spoken alarms.

Lock mode

Turn the phone into a clock for a focused night, a day off or a calendar event.

Private by design

No ads, no tracking, no account needed. Alarms, sounds and calendar stay on your phone. Optional sync only if you sign in.

Connected, if you want it

Quando? can sign in to your Google account to read your YouTube playlists and your Google Calendar (read-only), so you can pick a song or an event for an alarm. It uses that information only to show it to you inside the app. See the privacy policy for exactly what is collected and how to remove access.

Quando? is for Android and iPad, and coming to more devices.
