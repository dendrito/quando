Quando? is an alarm clock that knows when things actually happen. Set alarms around your meetings, sunrise and sunset, and the days you rest, then wake up the way you like.

Time well kept.

ALARMS AROUND YOUR DAY
• Normal alarms with repeat days, labels and groups, plus duplicate, turn off and undo.
• Calendar alarms: ring before, at or after any event in your phone's calendars, Google Calendar included. Search events by words, with AND, OR and NOT.
• Sunrise and sunset alarms for the city you choose.
• Several time zones and world clocks.

REST DAYS AND HOLIDAYS
• Lock mode: lock the phone down to the clock for a focused night, a day off or any calendar event.
• Hands-free alarms that ring for a set time and stop by themselves, with nothing to tap.
• Built-in Shabbat and Jewish holiday times for your city (Israel or diaspora), with more traditions to come.
• A reminder to charge your phone before your day off.

WAKE UP YOUR WAY
• Gentle nature sounds before the alarm: city, birds, waves, rain, a stream, wind, fire and night.
• Record your own sound or pick any audio file.
• Online radio from thousands of stations, by genre and country.
• Wake up to a song in Spotify or YouTube Music, with a backup sound if it doesn't start.
• Fade in and fade out for each alarm, or the same fades for every alarm.
• Choose exactly how long each alarm rings, to the second.

GET UP FOR REAL
• Snooze limits and spoken alarms.
• Stop by solving a sum, shaking the phone or scanning a code.
• A wake-up check a few minutes later.
• Snooze or stop with the volume buttons.

CONNECTED
• Send alarms to Pushover, Zapier, IFTTT or any webhook.
• "Ready to ring" checks your phone's settings so alarms always go off, with tips for your phone's brand.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads, no tracking, no account needed. Your alarms, sounds and calendar stay on your phone.
